# MarketPredictor v3.2.6 — 20-Strategy Research Replacement

This package preserves the original v3.2.5 application and replaces the prior 10-strategy intraday catalogue with 20 frozen baseline research strategies.

## Validation status

- Existing application structure preserved.
- Strategy engine now evaluates 20 modules in both LONG and SHORT directions (40 directional evaluations per scan).
- Existing selector, evidence weighting, EV gate, risk scaling, replay/live parity, persistence, and export layers are retained.
- 20-day and 252-day point-in-time swing levels are available to the strategy layer.
- Stock, market, and sector context are exposed to the strategy layer.
- Frozen baseline rules have not been optimized on historical results.
- No claim of profitable performance is made until real historical replay is run with the user's dataset and cost assumptions.

## Research caution

Some strategy names correspond to well-known strategy families. The baseline implementations are deterministic MarketPredictor implementations, not copied performance claims from their public sources. Published PF/Sharpe figures remain external research references and must not be treated as expected MarketPredictor results.
