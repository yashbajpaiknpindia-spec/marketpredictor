# MarketPredictor v3.4.21 — Shared Decision + Portfolio Engine

## Decision contract

Replay Research, Paper, and Live now share the same decision concepts:

1. Market context: regime, direction, volatility, relative strength, sector context, time bucket.
2. All 20 strategies are evaluated independently.
3. Each candidate receives context-conditioned historical expected **net** edge when evidence is available.
4. Candidates are ranked by expected net edge before capital allocation; confidence is only a secondary tie-breaker.
5. The shared finite-capital allocator reserves actual position notional and rejects candidates that cannot be funded.
6. Indian default portfolio capital is ₹2,00,000 (existing trading-capital configuration remains respected if explicitly changed).
7. Transaction-cost/slippage gates remain in the existing execution plan and the replay trade ledger stores cost-adjusted net P&L.
8. The hard execution loss ceiling is 0.125%; strategy-specific stops may be tighter, never wider.
9. Profit protection activation remains 0.40% and the existing validated trailing/protection logic is used by Replay/Paper/Live.

## Research Replay contract

The independent 20-strategy Research Replay remains capacity-free. It records eligible strategy opportunities rather than allowing finite capital to erase observations. Each newly generated research trade now carries its point-in-time context-conditioned expected net edge and calibrated win probability.

## ₹2,00,000 simulation contract

The second-stage simulation consumes the completed research ledger. At each timestamp, simultaneous candidates compete using the same shared portfolio allocator. Capital is reserved while positions are open and released at recorded exit with the replay's cost-adjusted net P&L. Older ledgers without expected-net-edge attribution are rejected rather than silently simulated with a different ranking rule.

## Exit correctness

The replay intrabar stop path uses the effective trailing/protective stop for the fill, not the original fixed stop, preventing protected winners from being falsely reported as exits at the old loss stop.
