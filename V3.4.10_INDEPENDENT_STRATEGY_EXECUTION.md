# V3.4.10 — Independent strategy execution

## Policy
- All 20 strategy definitions remain unchanged.
- Live and Paper intraday execution now default to `strategy_execution_mode=independent`.
- Every eligible strategy can produce its own candidate; no cross-strategy selector suppresses signals.
- Capital, position limits, same-strategy duplicate protection, cost gates, and the existing centralized exit engine remain account-level controls.
- Multiple strategies may hold the same ticker when independently signalled; the central position limit and capital guard constrain total exposure.
- The old selector path remains available as `strategy_execution_mode=selector` for backwards compatibility.

## UI
- Intraday dashboard displays **Strategy execution: Independent**.
- Candidate wording now describes independent strategy signals plus centralized risk/exits instead of a direction-aware selector.
