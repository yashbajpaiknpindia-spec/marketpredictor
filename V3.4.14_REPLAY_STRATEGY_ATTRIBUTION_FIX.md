# MarketPredictor v3.4.14 — Replay strategy attribution & per-strategy PF audit

## What was found in Replay #23

Replay #23's forensic trade records contained completed trades but no `strategy_id` attribution. That made `strategy_performance.json` and `strategy_audit.json` empty and prevented a trustworthy per-strategy PF/P&L report.

## Fixes

1. **All 20 strategies are always represented in Replay results.**
   - Evaluated count
   - Eligible count
   - Recorded completed trades
   - Win rate
   - Profit factor
   - Net P&L
   - Evidence/health state

2. **Strategy PF is now calculated from net rupee P&L**, not a sum of trade percentage returns. Transaction costs therefore affect the strategy PF consistently with account-level profitability.

3. **Zero-trade strategies are explicit.** They appear as `NO_TRADES_RECORDED` / `UNPROVEN`, rather than disappearing from the report.

4. **Attribution integrity is measured.** Replay reports total trades, attributed trades, unattributed trades, coverage %, and an overall `OK` / `ATTRIBUTION_GAP` status.

5. **Normal Replay entry events carry strategy attribution.** This makes the forensic event trail independently auditable against the completed trade records.

6. **Independent 20-strategy tournament results remain isolated.** Each strategy retains its own virtual ledger, and the exported batch PF is recomputed from the concatenated test trades instead of accidentally taking the last segmented child run's statistics.

7. **Forensic exports include the same complete per-strategy performance and attribution-integrity data.**

## Interpretation rule

A strategy with `Evaluated > 0` but `Recorded trades = 0` was tested but did not produce a completed trade after eligibility/execution gates. It must not be treated as either profitable or loss-making.

A strategy with recorded trades has its PF and net P&L computed only from trades carrying that strategy's attribution. Any unattributed completed trades are explicitly excluded from per-strategy evidence and counted in the attribution-gap diagnostic.
