# MarketPredictor v3.4.34.12

## Storage inventory and exact deletion fixes

- Fixed Storage Steward crash caused by treating opaque Historical Export `job_id` values (for example `def197ba12db`) as integers.
- Storage Steward now keeps archive/job identifiers as strings throughout duplicate/reclaim accounting.
- Storage page now shows the full inventory of Replay runs, Rapid Scan runs, and Market Data acquisition jobs with exact candidate ID, record name, dates/sessions, status, and individual delete action.
- Cleanup Center now has full-inventory actions instead of age/qualifying-only views. It reports every record in the selected category and never says `Nothing qualifies` when records exist.
- Exact deletion remains confirmation-gated and active jobs remain protected.
- Orphan coverage deletion now honors the exact selected candidate key rather than deleting every orphan row.
- Historical Export identifiers remain opaque strings and are never coerced to integers.
- Canonical 1-minute market data remains protected.
