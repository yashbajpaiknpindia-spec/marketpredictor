# MarketPredictor v3.4.20.4 — Replay / Capital Simulation Fix

## Changes
- Normal Research Replay remains an unlimited-capacity research pass; capital scarcity does not discard eligible signals.
- Fixed intrabar stop-side replay exits so an effective trailing stop is filled at the trailing protection price rather than the original fixed stop.
- Added a second-stage ₹2,00,000 capital simulation for completed/stopped replay runs. It consumes the completed replay trade ledger chronologically, reserves position notional while trades are open, applies the recorded cost-adjusted net P&L at exit, and reports funded/rejected trades, ending capital, return, PF and drawdown.
- Added the ₹2,00,000 simulation button to completed Replay results.
- Moved complete application-data Export/Import controls from Intraday Replay to Export Data.
- Preserved the configured stop-loss setting per replay; 0.125% remains a valid value. The current UI default is 0.6%.

## Important workflow
1. Run the normal Research Replay/backtest without a capital limit.
2. Inspect the normal replay statistics and trade ledger.
3. Only after completion, run the ₹2,00,000 Capital Simulation from that replay result.

The capital simulation is deliberately a second-stage portfolio test and does not change the research replay's signal generation.
