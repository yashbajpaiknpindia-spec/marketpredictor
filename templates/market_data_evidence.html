Smart-brain operating rulesevidence first, strategy rules fixed
1. Prove before promotingNo strategy is called “good” from a tiny sample. Promotion requires coverage, sample size and blind Replay.
2. Compare expectation vs realityEvery selected trade carries expected win probability, net edge and PF context; the audit computes the realized deltas.
3. Separate entry from exitHigh MFE with weak realized net and many exit failures points to management leakage rather than an automatic entry failure.
4. Never tune on the test windowThis page diagnoses failures. It does not alter the 20 strategies, thresholds or exits after seeing evaluation trades.
To increase winners scientifically, first identify whether the loss is caused by entry/context, payoff distribution, or exit timing. Then rerun a fresh blind holdout with the same strategy definitions. Any change must be specified before the new test, never inferred from the result and applied back into the same data.
