Microstructure glossary
The terms used by the live real-market collector and paper engine.
L1Best bid and best ask. The tightest visible market and the basis for spread/microprice.
L5Five visible bid levels and five ask levels. INDstocks' documented REST market-depth endpoint returns five levels.
L10 / 20 / 200Deeper order-book bands. They are useful for stronger microstructure research, but the current INDstocks documentation does not document 10/20/200-level delivery.
MicropriceA queue-weighted midpoint: it shifts toward the side with less displayed liquidity, giving a directional pressure clue.
Imbalance(bid depth − ask depth) ÷ (bid depth + ask depth). We calculate L1 and L5 versions.
OFIOrder-flow imbalance normally uses event-by-event adds, cancels and executions. Tomorrow we can only calculate a displayed-depth change proxy from snapshots.
SpreadAsk minus bid. A scalper should reject trades when the spread consumes too much of the expected move.
AbsorptionHeavy apparent aggressive pressure with weak price progress; it can signal hidden/replenishing liquidity and failed continuation.
Remaining edgeExpected gross move − move already consumed − execution friction. This is the anti-chase gate.

Research Lab checks the strategy on old market days before you trust it on new days. It does not show tomorrow’s picks. It shows what worked, what failed, and what the system should avoid or boost in future scans.
Backtest = past simulation only. It checks old market days, records what would have worked, and builds lessons for future scans. It does not show tomorrow’s stock list.
How much history is enough? More trades per pattern gives a more trustworthy win rate (roughly ±18% margin of error at 8 trades vs ±5% at 100 trades), but calendar length matters separately: a strategy also needs to be tested across a real bull run, a choppy/sideways stretch, and ideally a real correction/bear phase, not just one market mood. 3 years is a reasonable starting minimum; 5-10 years gives a better chance of covering multiple regimes, though very old data for some symbols may be thinner or less reliable.
Data correctness for splits/bonus issues: Backtests fetch each stock's corporate-action history alongside prices. A confirmed stock split or bonus issue (common on NSE/BSE) is detected and excluded both from the data-integrity check and from any simulated trade whose holding window would straddle it, so a real split cannot be mistaken for a fake crash or a fake stop-loss hit.
Raw vs Learned: Raw replays the base strategy exactly as coded, with no pattern-memory or per-stock history applied — this is the honest control/baseline. Learned re-scores and filters the same test days using every lesson the system has recorded so far (pattern penalties, regime weighting, per-stock strength). Use "Compare Raw vs Learned" to run both on the identical window and see whether learning actually improved the result, and by how much. There is no target of a perfect win rate here — a small, consistent, positive edge across many trades (profit factor above 1.0, held up across regimes) is the realistic goal.

How stock selection works: “Quality stocks to scan” means the starting tradable universe. The app first removes inactive, illiquid, old-symbol, low-volume, low-turnover, or bad-history stocks. Then, on each old test date, it ranks the remaining quality stocks by bullish setup, volume confirmation, risk/reward, market type, past pattern behavior, and stock-specific history. Rapid Scan controlsVerified real market data only · bounded CPU/memory · one parent run with parallel session lanes
Streaming mode: Rapid acquires one trading session, scans the verified real cells that are available, commits a durable checkpoint, then releases the session memory before moving on. A small real-data gap does not abort the session: missing ticker-days are skipped and recorded. The default minimum session coverage is 90%; 100% is still required for evidence-grade promotion. By default, candles newly downloaded by Rapid are deleted after the successful session checkpoint; pre-existing canonical candles are preserved. The run resumes from durable batch checkpoints after a web-service restart. Parallel waves are spread across the selected period so different months/period slices can run together. Existing verified ticker-days are reused, and no synthetic candles are used.
