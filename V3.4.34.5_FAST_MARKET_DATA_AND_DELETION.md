# MarketPredictor v3.4.34.5

- Fast 1m Market Data coverage inventory using the persisted coverage manifest and indexed cache metadata.
- Deep candle normalization is limited to suspect/unverified cells; verified cells are not re-deserialized on every preflight.
- Acquisition progress now exposes workflow progress, verified/expected coverage, elapsed time and ETA.
- Raw Rapid remains 1-minute canonical-data-only; 5-minute data remains in Historical Market Data export, not Rapid evidence.
- Replay cleanup invalidates corresponding market-data coverage rows so deleted cache cells cannot remain falsely marked COMPLETE.
- Added deletion/data-integrity audit endpoint and post-delete verification for Rapid, Replay and paper trade deletion paths.
- Rapid deletion explicitly removes associated Evidence snapshots/rows before deleting the run.
- Strategy definitions are unchanged. Strategy Dashboard ranking/metrics remain unchanged; Evidence/Live promotion stays gated by the Evidence Registry.
